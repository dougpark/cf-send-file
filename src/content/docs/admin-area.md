# Admin area and staff responsibilities

The **Admin** area is for authorized staff. What appears in the Admin menu depends on the roles assigned to your account. Signing in alone doesn't grant administrative access.

## Staff roles

- **Owner**: full control, including granting or revoking the Owner role. Only Owners can change other Owners.
- **Admin**: user management, access requests, permissions, and site settings.
- **Editor**: can open the Admin area to view Status and the Log Viewer. Extend this role for content-editing features in your app.

## Admin work areas

- **Status** shows counts of login accounts, members, staff, suspended accounts, alias emails, open access requests, and active users over the last day, week, month, and quarter.
- **User Management > Access Requests** lists requests from people without access. Approving one makes the person a member and emails them a sign-in link. Rejecting one emails them a notice. You can add internal review notes.
- **User Management > Login Accounts** lets you search for an account to grant or remove membership, suspend or reinstate it, view and revoke sessions, force a logout, email a sign-in link, or create a one-time sign-in link to copy.
- **User Management > Permissions** lets you grant or revoke Editor, Admin, and Owner roles. Type a full email address to add someone who hasn't signed in yet.
- **Log Viewer > Logs** shows the activity log, filtered by category or searched by action or email.
- **Log Viewer > Last Seen** lists the most recently active users.
- **Settings** controls the site name, the administrator contact email, and **Open sign-up**. With open sign-up on, anyone who signs in with a new email becomes a member automatically.

## Working safely

Check you've selected the right account before changing it, and wait for the success message. Every permission, membership, and settings change is recorded in the activity log. If a tool is missing, your account may not have that role; ask an Owner or Admin.
