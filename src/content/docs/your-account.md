# Managing your account

Open the account menu by selecting your name in the top right corner.

## Changing your display name

Choose **My Account**, edit **Display name**, and select **Save**. Your display name appears in the navigation bar and is what administrators see.

## Adding a sign-in alias email

In **My Account**, under **Sign-in alias emails**, select **Add email**, enter another address you'd like to sign in with (for example a work email), and select **Save**. You can then sign in with either address and reach the same account. Alias emails stay private. Remove an alias with the trash icon and save.

## Choosing a color theme

The account menu lists the available color themes. Select one to apply it right away. Your choice is remembered on that device.

## About this site

Choose **About** from the account menu to read a short overview of the site.
