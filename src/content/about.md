# About This Site
This site is built for simple, secure access on any device. Replace this page with a description of your app.

## Easy to Use

**Touch-Friendly Controls:** Large buttons and generous tap targets make navigation simple on phones, tablets, and computers.

**Clear, High-Contrast Text:** Accessible contrast ratios and clean typography keep text readable.

**Adaptive Color Themes:** Switch between light and dark themes from the account menu.

## Secure & Stress-Free Access

**Passwordless "Magic Link" Sign-In:** No passwords to remember. Sign in with a link or a six-digit code sent to your email.

**Extended Login Sessions:** Stay signed in on your trusted devices so you don't have to log in repeatedly.

**Members-Only Content:** Pages are only visible to approved members. Administrators review every new access request.

## Built-In Help

**Help Center:** Step-by-step guides for every part of the site.

**Help Assistant:** Ask a question in plain language and get an answer drawn from the help guides.
